Bookkeeping time

Logged is billable time bookkeepers explicitly entered or punched on a client's Time tab. Active is passively auto-tracked focus time (counted only while actively working inside a client; paused on idle or in the background). The two can overlap, so they are shown separately rather than summed.

Monthly report goals

These targets color the KPI box on every client's monthly report — a value at or above its goal shows green, below its floor shows red, and in between stays neutral. Sensible defaults are set; adjust to your firm's standards.

SharePoint folders

Which folder under Recurring Client Work belongs to which client. A client's Files tab shows its SharePoint folder only when the folder name matches the QuickBooks company name exactly (after ignoring case, punctuation and words like LLC / Inc / Services) or when you map it here by hand.

The app deliberately will not guess. Two different clients can look alike once those words are stripped — “Anderson Co” and “Anderson Plumbing LLC” both reduce to anderson… — and guessing wrong means one client's Files tab shows another client's statements, payroll and tax documents. Where a name is close but not identical, it is offered below as a suggestion for you to confirm.
